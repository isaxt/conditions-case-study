# Prototype 0.8 — the speculative end, in two steps

Files: `js/features/future.js`, `css/features/future.css`, `tests/future.js` (20 checks). Copy is in `COPY` at the top of future.js.

## Step 1 · after AI (era 2030; "✦ your agent" button)
Built from the visitor's own history:
- **Assembled for you**: must show / should show / never show, from what they carried and left behind (generative UI).
- **The dial**: how much the agent does. It starts from whether they took or refused the 2024 rewrite. At "drafts", the agent's words are marked and counted; at "asks", it writes nothing.
- **Errands**: the agent revisits the guestbook, the forum and the 1999 shop. The visitor decides whether it goes "as me" or says it is an agent (agentic web).
- **Who vouches**: their own things with a provenance chain; lost things show where the history was stripped; signing adds their name from 2030 on.
- **Something only you would get**: a hidden word taken from one of their answers.

## Step 2 · after the locks (era-bar button "after that ▸▸")
- **Opened today**: what they sent under the old locks (the gate they agreed to, early answers, the rewritten sentence) is readable; re-sealing protects it from now on only ("harvest now, decrypt later").
- **A note that can't be copied**: mote's note names what they carried; it can be read once; copying gives noise. They can leave one.
- **The cat came back**: two entangled cats settle together.
- **What comes home**: leads to the "yours" tab.

## Stance
Each panel states the gain first, then what it asks for. Nothing is a real agent, signature or lock; the window says so. The quantum parts are metaphors for two real properties (old secrets opened later; a state that cannot be copied), not predictions.
